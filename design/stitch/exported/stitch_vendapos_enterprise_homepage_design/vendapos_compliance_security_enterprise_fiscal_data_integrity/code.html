The Fiscal Engine

Every transaction signed, verified, and reconciled with KRA in milliseconds.

VendaPOS delivers real-time fiscalisation via direct eTIMS API microservices. Eliminate manual fiscal devices, prevent heavy tax penalties, and automate audit reconciliations across all your branch registers.

vpn_key

Real-time FDN Generation & Cryptographic Hash

The instant a sale completes, our microservice generates an authentic Fiscal Data Number (FDN) and signs an HMAC-SHA256 payload with your company’s certified KRA key pair. No batch delay, zero reconciliation gaps.

qr_code_scanner

Official KRA Verification QR Encoding

Every printed and digital customer receipt renders an official KRA public verification QR code. Customers or tax enforcement officers can scan using any smartphone camera to inspect the ledger entry directly on the KRA portal.

signal_disconnected

Deterministic Offline Fiscal Spooling

Internet dropouts do not halt transactions. When connectivity fails, the register signs transactions locally within a tamper-proof hardware buffer. Sequential timestamps are maintained and cryptographically submitted immediately when uplink restores.

receipt_long

Automated Midnight Z-Report Reconciliation

End-of-day register balancing runs autonomously. The cloud controller computes physical cash, M-Pesa STK settlements, and card drafts, auto-matching each register's totals against the KRA fiscal registry to produce discrepancy-free daily audit packs.

Payments security with zero merchant exposure.

We decouple payment collection from merchant data liabilities. Neither cardholder primary account numbers (PAN) nor unverified mobile money tokens ever exist inside your business network.

contactless PCI-DSS Scoping Shield

Card Payments (Zero Touch Architecture)

Card data never touches, transits, or resides on VendaPOS servers or your local store terminals.

Direct tokenization is executed directly on hardware PIN pads certified by Level 1 PCI-DSS partner gateways (including Pesapal, DPO Group, and Stanbic Bank). The cashier terminal receives only a cryptographically blind authorization token.

  • check_circle P2PE Hardware Encryption: PIN and magnetic/EMV data encrypted at physical reader head.
  • check_circle Tokenized Vault Routing: Card credentials never written to RAM or local disk storage.
  • check_circle Automated Chargeback Audit Logs: Immutable bank settlement traces mapped to till operator PINs.
verified Finance Outcome: Eliminates 95% of SAQ-D PCI audit requirements for multi-store chains.
phonelink_lock Anti-Fraud Native

M-Pesa STK Push (Server-Side Verification)

Direct C2B and B2B Safaricom Daraja API integration with cryptographically validated webhooks.

Eliminates the #1 retail fraud vector in East Africa: fabricated SMS confirmations and photoshopped customer confirmation screenshots. The cashier never inspects the patron's smartphone.

  • check_circle Zero Cashier Trust Loop: Till drawer opens only after Safaricom returns an HTTP 200 payload.
  • check_circle Mutual-TLS (mTLS) Webhooks: Webhook payload signed via asymmetric certificates to prevent replay attacks.
  • check_circle Exact Amount Match Enforcement: Register locks until the inbound push matches the bill down to the cent.
verified Finance Outcome: Zero shrinkage from forged M-Pesa codes or unreflected merchant reversions.
