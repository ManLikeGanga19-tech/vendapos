Everything you need to know about restaurant deployment.

Yes, 100%. VendaPOS is built offline-first using embedded local database engines on every terminal. If Safaricom fiber or mobile networks cut out, table ordering, kitchen KDS routing, receipt printing, and cash register kicks continue without interruption. When connectivity restores, transactions automatically synchronize upstream to the central cloud.
When a table splits a bill, the waiter selects the individual guest items or specifies custom amount splits on the handheld screen. VendaPOS immediately sends separate Safaricom STK prompt requests to each guest's respective phone number. Each payment verifies independently in real time, and the register will only close the table once the full balance is covered.

A single order containing cocktails, cold appetizers, and charcoal choma will instantly split: drink chits print at the bar dispense station, appetizers show on the pantry KDS, and grilled meats display on the kitchen pass with course tags.
Yes. VendaPOS natively embeds the KRA eTIMS Type-C fiscal transmission engine. Every thermal checkout receipt prints with a valid cryptographic QR code, Fiscal Day Number (FDN), and Invoice Number transmitted automatically to KRA systems. You avoid secondary fiscal boxes or manual portal typing.
